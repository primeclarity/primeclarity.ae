Start with a conversation

Every engagement begins with a conversation. We learn how your business actually runs — how you sell, invoice, pay and report — and where your current system is letting you down.

01Discuss

Next, we gather what's needed: trade licence, bank statements, invoices, bills and your VAT or Corporate Tax registrations. Everything is received securely and checked before anything is built.

02Collect

With the full picture, we recommend Zoho Books, Xero or QuickBooks and configure it around you — chart of accounts, VAT, bank feeds, users and approval workflows.

03Configure

Your history comes across carefully. Opening balances are migrated, mapped and reconciled line by line, so the new books agree with the old before you switch over.

04Migrate & Reconcile

We switch you over and train the people who will use it — owners, finance staff and sales — so the system is used properly from the first day.

05Go Live & Train

From there, the numbers start working for you: monthly management reports, cash-flow forecasts and KPIs, backed by ongoing accounting, compliance and CFO-level advice.

Zoho Books, Xero & QuickBooks Consulting in Abu Dhabi

Prime Clarity is a finance and accounting consultancy based in Abu Dhabi, helping local businesses run their accounting on Zoho Books, Xero and QuickBooks. We work with owner-managed companies and SMEs across the emirate that have outgrown spreadsheets, manual bookkeeping or a system that was never set up correctly — and want a finance function they can actually rely on.

Our core work is cloud accounting implementation on Zoho Books, Xero and QuickBooks: helping you choose the right platform, setting it up properly, mapping your chart of accounts, configuring VAT, structuring records for Corporate Tax, connecting banking and building the reporting that gives owners real visibility. For businesses already on one of these platforms, we review and fix setups that aren't delivering — cleaning up reconciliation, reporting and workflows so the system does what it should.

Beyond the software, we provide the accounting behind it: bookkeeping, VAT accounting, Corporate Tax support and financial reporting, plus CFO advisory when you're ready to move from clean books to sharper decisions — cash-flow forecasting, budgeting, KPIs and profitability analysis.

We support businesses across mainland Abu Dhabi and its business communities — from Mussafah and KIZAD to Khalifa City, Al Raha and Al Reem Island. Because these platforms are cloud-based, we can also support companies elsewhere in the UAE, but our focus and presence are here in Abu Dhabi. If you want modern cloud finance with real accounting and UAE tax expertise behind it, that's exactly what we do.

FAQ

Accounting software & finance — common questions

Which accounting software do you work with?
We work with Zoho Books, Xero and QuickBooks — cloud platforms for invoicing, expenses, banking, VAT and reporting. We implement and configure your chosen platform correctly for how your business runs, then keep the accounting behind it accurate.
Should we choose Zoho Books, Xero or QuickBooks?
It depends on your size, industry, how you invoice, the integrations you need and who uses the system. We walk you through the trade-offs in a free consultation and recommend the platform that fits — we are not tied to any one of them.
Are Zoho Books, Xero and QuickBooks suitable for UAE businesses?
Yes. Each can be configured for UAE VAT and set up to keep clean records for Corporate Tax. We configure the tax settings, chart of accounts and reporting to fit UAE requirements.
Can you migrate our accounting from Tally to Zoho Books, Xero or QuickBooks?
Yes. We handle Tally migration — chart-of-accounts mapping, opening balances and validation — so your history carries over cleanly.
Can you migrate our accounts from Excel to cloud accounting software?
Yes. Moving from Excel or manual bookkeeping to Zoho Books, Xero or QuickBooks is one of the most common projects we run for Abu Dhabi SMEs.
Can you configure VAT in Zoho Books, Xero or QuickBooks?
Yes. We set VAT up correctly and support accurate return preparation and reconciliation.
Can you help with UAE Corporate Tax accounting?
Yes. We keep records structured for Corporate Tax and assist with computations and compliance.
We already use Zoho Books, Xero or QuickBooks. Can you review our setup?
Yes. We run a health check across your chart of accounts, VAT setup, reconciliation, workflows and reporting, then fix what isn't working.
Do you provide ongoing accounting after implementation?
Yes. Implementation is the start — we can continue as your accounting and finance partner with bookkeeping, reconciliations, reporting, tax support and CFO advisory.
Can Prime Clarity provide CFO advisory?
Yes. Once your finance function runs properly, we provide CFO-level support: cash-flow forecasting, budgeting, KPIs, profitability and management reporting.
Do you work with businesses outside Abu Dhabi?
Our focus is Abu Dhabi, but because Zoho Books, Xero and QuickBooks are cloud-based we can support businesses elsewhere in the UAE too.
How much does accounting software implementation cost?
It depends on your size, transaction volume and how much migration and accounting support you need. Book a free consultation and we'll scope it with you.
